A LITTLE LESS TO THINK ABOUT

Park. Remember.
Pass it on.

From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, keep the useful details in one place.

01 / ARRIVE

Make it your spot.

Tap Park Here to save your car’s location. Optional automatic detection can help recognize parking and departure with the permissions you choose.

02 / RETURN

Skip the guessing.

Open My Car to see where you parked and get directions back. Add a private note, garage details, or photo for the things a map can’t tell you.

03 / LEAVE

Help someone next.

Choose to share a parking space when you leave. Other drivers can see community reports nearby and check whether a spot is still open.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you hit
the road.

Is a reported parking spot guaranteed?

No. Community reports indicate possible availability and can change quickly. Always check the space, signs, and local parking restrictions.

How does automatic detection work?

When enabled, ParkSpoting uses location and activity signals to help detect parking and departure. Background detection depends on permissions, device settings, and operating-system conditions. You can always save a spot manually.

Do I need to use the app while driving?

No interaction is required while driving. Set up your preferences before a trip and use the app only when it is safe to do so.

Where can I download ParkSpoting?

ParkSpoting is currently in testing. Join the Android test, then open its Google Play listing to download. An App Store download link will be added when the iOS app is available.
